1. Title

Digital Products Marketplace

2. Domain

 Digital Marketplace

3. Who is the user?

* Admin
* Seller
* Customer

4. What problem are we solving?

Creators find it difficult to sell digital products securely. Buyers also need a trusted platform to purchase genuine digital products. This project connects both safely.

5. Proposed Solution

* User login
* Upload digital products
* Search products
* Buy products
* Secure payment
* Download after purchase
* Ratings and reviews

6. Core Entities / Database Tables

* Users
* Products
* Categories
* Orders
* Payments
* Reviews

7. User Roles & Permissions

Admin: Manage users, products, and sellers.
Seller: Upload and manage products.
Customer: Buy, download, and review products.

8. Success Criteria

Users can register, buy products, make payments, and download products successfully.

9. Out of Scope

* Live chat
* Mobile app
* Cryptocurrency payment
* Subscription plans

10. Chosen Track

Python (Django)
